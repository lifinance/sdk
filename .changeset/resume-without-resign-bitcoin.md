Mark a cancelled replacement as final so "Try again" signs a new transaction; any other failure after broadcast is re-checked instead. The sign task refuses to sign while the step has an open transaction. The signed transaction (`txHex`, `txHash`, `signedAt`) is now stored before it is sent, so a reload during the send resumes it instead of signing again. A first send that every configured node refuses for a reason that proves none of them holds it (consensus or standardness rules, a fee below its floor, or a full mempool), and that no node knows by txid, clears it, so "Try again" signs anew; any other send failure is re-checked, and the stored transaction is resent on a resume (a reload or "Try again") only within two minutes of signing. If the first send's outcome stays unknown and no resend happens within two minutes of signing, a resume only waits; the wait ends after about ten blocks with an error that is not final, and the exit is to delete the route.
